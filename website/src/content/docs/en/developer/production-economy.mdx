Canwu provides the deterministic infrastructure needed by a production economy, but it does not put grain, factories, prices, historical resource deposits, or armies into the engine core.

## Recommended boundary

- Canwu core owns command admission, deterministic time, phase ordering, reservation allocation, atomic commit, rollback, visibility, persistence, and replay.
- The host domain owns `ResourceAccount`, `ResourceDemand`, `ResourceReservation`, `ResourceTransfer`, and consumption settlement.
- A host transport adapter owns cargo balance, in-transit custody, loss, arrival acceptance, and destination credit. `canwu-transport` supplies movement execution, capacity booking, itinerary legs, handoffs, and the delivery-completion bridge.
- Population, production, and military plugins form demands and consequences separately; they do not directly rewrite one another's stock.

This keeps one authority: the host registers typed records and plugin boundary systems through the public `canwu-api`, and every write is checked against its declared owner/system before atomic commit.

## Minimal lifecycle

1. Demands enter through admission; admission does not directly write stock.
2. The resource owner publishes a protected-floor-aware `ReservationOffer`; consumers submit attempt-local `ReservationRequest` values.
3. Boundary systems read deterministic allocation evidence and propose only their own ledger, transport, or consequence records.
4. Conservation, version, custody-predecessor, or authority failure rolls back the whole proposal group.
5. A destination is credited only after the cargo disposition is accepted; military consumption becomes eligible at the next appropriate boundary.

The account amount is the only writable quantity. Reserved, available, and protected views are derived; reach is not delivery and arrival is not consumption.

## Versioning and saves

Hosts pin the `canwu-api` version and plugin semantic hashes. Loading restores the same Canwu snapshot and revalidates host typed records; it must not rebuild an old campaign from its current timestamp or treat an unpublished local engine version as a compatible API.

See the [production, resource, and facility mechanism proposal](/en/proposals/production-economy-mechanism/) for candidate extensions and the boundary of what should not enter the core.
