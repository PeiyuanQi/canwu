## Decision

**Status: delivered in 0.10.0.** Production, consumption, and military supply remain optional extensions above the public `canwu-api`. Grain, factories, prices, armies, and historical resource maps do not enter the simulation core.

A historical Celestial Mandate fixture first validated the boundary against the Canwu 0.6 public API. The 0.10.0 G1b slice now runs a fourteen-month grain loop with real `canwu-routing` and `canwu-transport` types. The G3 production extension and G4 independent force-supply consumer prove that two different downstream lifecycle owners reuse the same resource boundary.

The extraction gate is therefore satisfied: `canwu-resource` and `canwu-production` are publishable optional extensions, while `canwu-force-supply-reference` and `canwu-economy-reference` remain replaceable integrations. G5 supplies evidence-qualified local scarcity and price-pressure reads, not a full market simulation.

## Ownership

- The simulation core owns admission, time and phase ordering, reservation allocation, declared reads and writes, atomic commit, rollback, evidence, visibility, persistence, and replay.
- `canwu-resource` owns resource definitions, units, accounts, protected floors, demands, reservations, allocation, transfers, consumption, loss, and fulfillment.
- `canwu-production` owns processes, production sites, facilities, capacity, work orders, work in progress, execution, projects, and output settlement.
- `canwu-transport` supplies movement execution, itinerary legs, capacity, custody handoff, and the delivery-completion bridge. The resource extension remains the authority for in-transit cargo balance, loss, acceptance, return, and destination credit.
- Population, production, and military domains form demands and consume exact fulfillment outcomes. They do not rewrite another domain's state.
- Historical content owns places, deposits, processes, facilities, institutions, parameters, and provenance. It receives no privileged mutation path.

## Satisfied extraction gate

G3 production and G4 force supply are independent lifecycle owners that consume the same account, reservation, allocation, transfer, and fulfillment semantics. Production sits above the conserved layer, owns versioned processes, work orders, work in progress, maintenance, and facilities, and returns output to the resource extension for credit.

This delivery adds no simulation-core query API and moves no domain semantics into the core. A future full market, money, property, universal construction, or combat system still needs independent-consumer evidence and a failing public-API fixture; a diagram alone cannot grant it a core position.

## Content that stays out of the simulation core

- Historical goods, deposits, agricultural calendars, and era parameters.
- A national price used as physical stock truth.
- Building levels, production methods, or technology unlocks that automatically create local capability.
- Treating route reach as booking, departure, arrival, or consumable delivery.
- Treating transport execution records as the authority for host cargo balance and loss.
